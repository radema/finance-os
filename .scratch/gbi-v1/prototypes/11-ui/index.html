Goal-based investing: UI direction

Screens built on palette D and the OE exhibit rules, with the mock’s sample data (valuation date 31 Oct 2026). Nothing is saved. Add ?theme=dark or use the toggle. The layout is fluid from 390 to 1920 px: sidebar from 960 px, a menu on phones. Notes: NOTES.md.

1 · Overview (Dashboard)One message first, then a grid of charts: chance of success, net worth by goal, trend, range of outcomes, savings capacity, independence.One colour per goal, price freshness, plain-word status, jargon popovers.
2 · Goal detailRange of outcomes, what it would take, holdings with price age. Switch goals with the chips, or open Long-term investment. The goals list is its own page.
3 · Transactions and importThe list as rows on phones, and the import in four steps: paste or upload, match columns, check rows, import.
4 · First runThe empty Overview as a checklist: products, goals, what you hold today. Deep links: products, goals.
5 · Financial independenceThree levels, how far along you are, and the projection. Tick the goals that count.
6 · PlaceholderScreens in the navigation that are not built (Optimizer, Pension, Prices, Setup, …) lead to one placeholder page.
