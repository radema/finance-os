Products the optimizer may use

How the optimizer chooses

  1. It builds every mix of the products you tick, in steps of 10% (20% with 5–6 products), respecting the maximum per product.
  2. It runs each mix through the same 300 simulated markets, so differences come from the mix, not from luck.
  3. It keeps the mixes that reach the goal’s required probability, then picks one with the criterion below. If none reach it, it shows the mix with the highest probability.
CriterionPicks the mix that…Good for
Protect the bad casehas the highest P10: in 9 markets out of 10 you end up with at least thisNeed goals (default)
Grow the typical outcomehas the highest median: half the markets end above itWant and Wish goals (default)
Spend leastreaches the required probability with the lowest monthly contributiona tight budget
Maximise the chancehas the highest probability of reaching the target, whatever the mediangoals that are behind

The result is only as good as your return and volatility assumptions: optimizers exaggerate their errors. Cap the weight per product to avoid extreme mixes.
