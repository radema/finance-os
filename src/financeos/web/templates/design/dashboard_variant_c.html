{# =========================================================================== VARIATION C — «Il patrimonio annotato» (PROTOTYPE, W2 #12) =========================================================================== AGGREGATE FIRST. Net worth and the composition of the whole lead the page, and the findings arrive as an annotation layer hanging off it: each Bucket carries what was measured inside it, what was not, and its worst severity. The ordered list of findings is a compact ledger below, not the subject. FIRST QUESTION: what is held, and in which part of it are the deviations? THIS IS THE VARIATION UX-0001 §5.3 ARGUES AGAINST, deliberately and in full. §5.3 says there is no net-worth hero number because P1 makes the decisions the product, so the landing screen is the findings list. That section also says of itself that it is "a reading of the Manifest, not a requirement" (UX-0001:155), and the product owner asked for a dashboard on 2026-08-01. So the argument is not settled by fiat here — it is built, with its cost visible, so the owner can price it. If this variation is picked, §5.3's row becomes a pointer per UX-0001 §10 and nothing needs overturning: P1 is the scope ratification, not a landing-page rule. WHAT MAKES IT SURVIVABLE ANYWAY. Two things, and without them it would be the false-reassurance dashboard §5.3 fears: 1. NO BUCKET SHOWS A FINDING COUNT WITHOUT ITS UNEVALUATED COUNT. Personale is the case that proves it: zero findings AND seven rules that never ran, shown together on the same card. A card reading "0 rilievi" alone would be ADR-0016's silent false reassurance re-introduced at the presentation layer, after the domain went to trouble to prevent it. 2. THE WHOLE RECONCILES IN VIEW. The composition segments close on the net worth tick, and the unassigned residual is one of them. A hero number whose parts are not shown closing on it is a number nobody can check. THE COST, stated in the head block: the first thing the product says becomes a total rather than a deliberation. STRUCTURALLY: hero, then a card grid, then a compact ledger. No records. #} {# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the whole #}
