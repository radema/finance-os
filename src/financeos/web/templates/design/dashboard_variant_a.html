{# =========================================================================== VARIATION A — «Il verbale della valutazione» (PROTOTYPE, W2 #12) =========================================================================== COVERAGE FIRST. The page reads top-to-bottom as one evaluation's report: what the run measured, what it could not measure, then the findings in the order mv_finding gives them, then the holdings as closing context. FIRST QUESTION: can this run be trusted, and what did it not measure? THE ARGUMENT. ADR-0016 says silent false reassurance is the failure mode most likely to mislead. Every other conceivable dashboard puts findings or a total first and the coverage somewhere below the fold — which is precisely the presentation-layer version of the bug the four-outcome model exists to prevent. This variation inverts that: the census of outcomes is the first thing on the page, and the findings are read *inside* the coverage they came from. WHAT IT DELIBERATELY REFUSES. No net-worth figure above the findings, and no aggregate "salute" score. A single score over four outcomes that mean different things would be the average ADR-0016 and RFC-0007 §5 both forbid in their own domains. THE COST, stated in the head block and not hidden: a reader who opens the product to see what the portfolio is worth reads a run report instead, and still has to go to /allocation. STRUCTURALLY: one column, no cards, no grid. Density over navigation. #} {# ---------------------------------------------------------------- 01 census #}
