{# PROTOTYPE SCAFFOLDING — the variation switcher. Not part of the design system. Deliberately styled to look like an instrument bolted onto the page rather than part of it: the owner must never mistake the switcher for a component under evaluation. It is high-contrast, hard-edged, and it names itself. It is a row of plain links, so it works with JavaScript off and every variation has a shareable, reload-stable URL. The arrow-key binding is a convenience on top, and it does not fire while a text field has focus.
