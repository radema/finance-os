{# =========================================================================== CONTRACT 4a — OUTCOME CHIPS (UX-0001 §4.4) =========================================================================== Four outcomes, four distinct treatments. SKIPPED and ERROR are NEVER styled as reassurance. ADR-0016 identifies silent false reassurance as the failure mode most likely to mislead the user. A UI that renders "0 rilievi" over 20 skipped rules re-introduces it at the presentation layer, after the domain went to trouble to prevent it. TEXTURE, NOT HUE, carries the distinction — solid / hairline / dashed / hatched. The four stay unmistakable in greyscale, in forced-colors, and to a red-green colour-blind reader. That matters more than usual here because the two most consequential states in this product are conventionally coded red and green. Colour only reinforces; the label always states the outcome. --------------------------------------------------------------------------- INTERFACE --------------------------------------------------------------------------- outcome str "FIRED" | "NOT_FIRED" | "SKIPPED" | "ERROR" (the stored enum member, English, uppercase) reason str|None REQUIRED whenever outcome is SKIPPED or ERROR — the skip reason or error kind, e.g. "zero_portfolio_value", "heterogeneous_strategies", "currency_mismatch".
