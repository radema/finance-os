ADR-L03 §6 elevated this from decorative to LOAD-BEARING: once the chrome and the user's prose are both Italian, typography is the ONLY remaining signal separating the system's arithmetic from the user's own words. The system never speaks in serif. Nothing else may borrow the serif face.
