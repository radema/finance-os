This is P3 and P6 made visible: a reader can always tell whether they are being shown the system's arithmetic or a restatement of their own prior declaration. Under ADR-0023 the rationale is assembled FROM the user's frozen strategy — the typography says so without needing a caption. ADR-L03 §6 elevated this from decorative to LOAD-BEARING: once the chrome and the user's prose are both Italian, typography is the ONLY remaining signal separating the system's arithmetic from the user's own words. The system never speaks in serif. Nothing else may borrow the serif face. This file is the legend, rendered in the rail foot — a reader who has not been told what the three faces mean cannot use the distinction they encode.
