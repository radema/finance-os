FINANCE OS — VENDORED TYPEFACES
===============================

Every face this application ships, and nothing else. ADR-0008 Local First: no
font is fetched from a network host, ever, so this directory is the complete
list of type in the product and it must stay that way.

All three families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.
The full licence text is in LICENSE-IBM-Plex.txt in this directory; OFL 1.1 is
one licence text and it governs all three, so the per-family copyright notices
and Reserved Font Names it requires are recorded here.

All three are LATIN SUBSETS. They cover Italian accents, the euro sign, the
middle dot, plus/minus and the true minus, and the single guillemets. A glyph
outside that range must be DRAWN IN CSS, never typed — widening a subset for one
character is how a 340 KB font budget becomes a 900 KB one. The tolerance rail's
clamp caret is drawn as a CSS triangle for exactly this reason.

WHY THESE THREE (see /DESIGN.md, Typography):
  Three families, not one superfamily. They do not share metrics, so each voice
  carries its own size and leading step — and in exchange a change of voice is
  unmistakable, which is what UX-0001 §4.5 and ADR-L03 §6 make load-bearing.
  Martian Mono was on the table as the value voice and was declined: its advance
  width is roughly 40% wider than Plex Mono's, and the value voice carries the
  trace ladder, which is the densest text in the product.

FACES REMOVED when World B shipped (they served the rejected identity or a
candidate proof, and Local First means an unused face is dead weight in the
repo, not a harmless spare): ibm-plex-sans-var, ibm-plex-serif-400,
ibm-plex-serif-400-italic, faustina-400-italic, martian-mono-400,
martian-mono-500.

KNOWN GAP, inherited and worth closing: these files carry no integrity pin. The
vendored ECharts bundle records a sha256 in vendor/echarts/PINNED.txt because a
CDN silently resolved a version during vendoring; fonts deserve the same
treatment, and do not have it yet.
