- **`3ef52e1`** — **ADR-L04**: `over_apportioned` severity, closing O3.

### ADR-L04 in one paragraph

`severity = 1 + Σdeficit / Σapportioned` = `2 − coverage`, aggregated over affected assets: `1.00`
exactly at the firing threshold, `2.00` when the position is fully sold out, range `(1,2]`, total,
expressible in the existing step algebra with no bound literal. O3's naive `1 + deficit/holding` was
rejected on a defect rather than on taste: it keeps ADR-0025's denominator, which is **zero in the
commonest firing state** (a full exit) — and a zero denominator does not yield a null severity, it
raises `SkipSignal(ZERO_DENOMINATOR)` (`rule_engine.py:466`), so the finding would disappear into run
health instead of firing.

**Fixture consequence:** UX-0001 §8.5 row 9 severity `0.15` → **`1.13`** (stored `1.13207547`), and
**row order moves** — `over_apportioned` 9→8, `drift GOV_EUR·Pension` (1.05) 8→9. All three `ORDER BY`
clauses stay exercised; the `NULLS LAST` relocation from `HANDOFF-2026-07-26.md` §3.3 is untouched and
now permanent. The synthetic ordering test owes two additions: priority dominance at a tiny margin,
and a null sorting below `1.00`.
