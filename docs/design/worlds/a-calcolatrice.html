La Calcolatrice

Olivetti · Pintori · la macchina da calcolo italiana

The interface as a well-made Italian calculating machine: Olivetti's design programme, which existed to make computation legible and humane. Flat poster inks on warm near-white, hard-edged marks, no gradients, generous air, geometric grotesque set tight. One vermilion warning lamp and nothing else that shouts.

Perché questo prodotto. The only cultural world where Italian design and computation are already the same subject. It gives the product an idiom for being precise without being cold, and for treating a number as something a machine produced and a person must read. In-tolerance is NEUTRAL here, not green: the machine reports, it does not congratulate — which is the Manifest's refusal to reassure, rendered as palette.

Grafici. Flat poster blocks with hard edges. The target is a heavy ink rule crossing the bar, so "where you are" and "where you said you'd be" are one glance and one axis. Category labels in wide mono.

Elevazione. none — hairlines and flat colour only. Allarme riservato: #c1330f chiaro / #f0714a scuro, mai usato come colore di serie.
