Il Mosaico

Ravenna · tessere · un intero fatto di parti contate

Byzantine-Italian mosaic. A warm bone ground, tesserae in lapis, malachite, pavonazzo, sky and gold, and every quantity drawn as discrete counted units separated by grout. Nothing is a smooth length; everything is a count.

Perché questo prodotto. The strongest fit to the domain's actual arithmetic. This product's numbers are sums of countable things — units apportioned across Buckets, evaluations in a run, band-widths of drift — and a mosaic bar lets the reader COUNT rather than compare lengths. It is also the honest picture of a whole composed of parts that must reconcile, which is what net worth equals buckets plus UNASSIGNED actually means.

Grafici. Every bar is built from discrete tesserae with visible grout, so allocation is counted, not measured. The target is a gold tessera set into the row at its declared position.

Elevazione. none — grout gaps and tonal steps carry every separation. Allarme riservato: #a83521 chiaro / #e07158 scuro, mai usato come colore di serie.
